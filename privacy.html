Privacy policy: rclone Drive backup

This app is a personal backup tool used only by its owner, Douglas Lalonde.

What it accesses

It signs in to the owner's own Google account and reads files from the owner's own Google Drive.

Where the data goes

Those files are copied only to the owner's own private Amazon S3 storage. The app does not collect, share, sell, or transmit any data to any other person, company, or service, and no data is used for advertising.

Login tokens

Google login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
