rclone Drive backup

This is a personal backup tool made and used only by its owner, Douglas Lalonde. It signs in to the owner's own Google account, reads files from the owner's own Google Drive, and copies them to the owner's own private Amazon S3 storage.

It is not offered to the public and it has no other users. It does not collect, share, or sell any data.
